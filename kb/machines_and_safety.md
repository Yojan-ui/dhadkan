## Who decides

Dhadkan only listens and advises. No software here can stop, slow or switch a machine. When a fault looks
serious, the owner is asked whether to stop; the owner, or a person they trust on the floor, decides and does
it. Anything that costs money (a mechanic's visit, a part) waits for the owner's yes, which is logged.

## Safe working for hand checks

A hand check is done only with the machine switched off at its isolator, the isolator locked or tagged so
nobody switches it on, and the moving parts fully stopped. Belt and coupling guards are removed only by a
person trained for it and put back before the machine is started. Electrical tests are for a qualified
electrician. Hot surfaces are touched with the back of the hand first.

## Pumps

Centrifugal pumps in small factories move water for washing, cooling and boilers. Their usual faults are
cavitation from a blocked suction, seal leaks, bearing wear and impeller wear. A pump's sound changes with the
water level and the valve position, so its normal recording should be taken at its usual duty.

## Fans and blowers

Fans and blowers in flour mills and dust extraction carry dust and flour that builds up on the blades. Their
usual faults are imbalance, bearing wear and belt slip. A blower that changes its sound after cleaning needs a
new normal recording.

## Gearboxes and looms

Gearboxes, powerlooms and other speed reducers have meshing gears whose tone rises with wear and with poor
lubrication. A change in the gear-mesh tone after an oil change or a load change is normal; a steady rise over
days is not. Keep the oil at the level and grade the maker gives.

## Valves and sliders

Solenoid valves and linear sliders work in cycles. Their sound is judged per cycle: a valve that chatters, a
slider that knocks at its end stop, or a cycle that takes longer than before points to wear, a loose stop or
poor lubrication.

## Recording a good sample

Hold the phone at the same place each time, close to the bearing housing but not touching moving parts, for 10
seconds while the machine runs at its usual load. Keep other loud machines in the background the same as when
the normal was recorded.
