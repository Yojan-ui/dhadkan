SURAKSHA GENERAL INSURANCE CO. LTD. (sample)
MACHINERY BREAKDOWN INSURANCE - POLICY SCHEDULE

Policy no: MB/2026/PN/004512
Insurer: Suraksha General Insurance Co. Ltd.
Insured: Shinde Engineering Works, Bhosari MIDC, Pune
Policy period from: 01-04-2026
Policy period to: 31-03-2027
Machine: Powerloom SGL-56 with 2 HP motor, serial no SGL56-2025-04471
Sum insured: Rs. 1,80,000
Deductible: Rs. 5,000 per claim

Scope of cover:
- Sudden and unforeseen physical damage to the insured machine
- Electrical breakdown, including motor burn-out and winding failure
- Gearbox breakdown
- Cost of repair or replacement and labour

Exclusions:
- Gradual wear and tear, rust and slow deterioration
- Consumable parts: belts, pickers, shuttles, lubricants
- Loss of production
- Damage while the machine is under the manufacturer's warranty, to the extent the manufacturer pays

Conditions:
- Do not repair or dismantle the machine before the surveyor inspects it, except to prevent further damage
- Keep the damaged parts for the surveyor

How to claim:
1. Intimate the claim within 48 hours on the claims helpline or by email.
2. Give the policy no., the machine serial no. and what happened.
3. A surveyor visits and inspects the machine.
4. Submit the repair estimate, the final bill and photos.
5. The claim is paid to your bank account after the deductible.
